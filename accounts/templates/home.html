FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to some common questions about the online registration process.

How do I register for a medical licence?

Create a health worker account, sign in, complete the medical licence application and upload the required documents.

Can I save my application before submitting?

Yes. You can save your application as a draft and return later to complete and submit it.

How can I track my application?

After signing in, your dashboard displays the current status of your application.

How will I receive my medical licence?

Once your application has been approved, your digital medical licence becomes available from your dashboard.
